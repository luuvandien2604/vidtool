## 3. Safety & Invariants

1. **AI Proposes, Editorial Engine Decides**:
   The AI Director outputs an advisory `EditorialIntent`. The deterministic `StrategyPlanner` evaluates candidates and retains final authority over strategy selection.
2. **Untrusted AI Output**:
   No pixel coordinates, ASS styles, FFmpeg filters, or OS commands can be generated by or accepted from the AI.
3. **Hard Constraint Dominance**:
   Visual family streak limits (`max_family_streak = 2`) strictly override any AI preference.
4. **Resilience & Fallback**:
   Any LLM failure (timeout, network error, invalid JSON, or rejection by the validator) immediately falls back to deterministic catalog defaults without disrupting pipeline execution.
